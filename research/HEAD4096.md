# Stream the head in 4096-row weight chunks

Runtime control: `77cfc4d7ef293b1d45e2dab06b5428d178f1faa1`, 28-layer
offload and bulk activation H2D. Its first official solution runs are
62.52/62.57 s /495 MiB; checks are pending at experiment preparation time.

The baseline trace attributes 1.400 GB H2D over three steps to the head, about
19% of all H2D bytes. Head forward GPU activities sum to 195 ms in those steps;
sum durations overlap and do not equal a removable part of the critical path.
Head statistics alone are small, so do not implement a speculative stats fusion.

Change only the head chunk to 4096 and compile those actual full/tail statistics
layouts in the same measured process. Two GPU head buffers shrink from 48 to
24 MiB. This doubles head chunk count and may lose through call overhead or
matmul shape efficiency. Smaller transfers might improve overlap. Both effects
require a cold measurement; no gain is assumed. Arithmetic/outputs follow the
same streaming algorithm, with a different number of FP32 accumulation chunks.

This is separate from the rejected 16384-row variant, which was slower and
larger. Full original loss/entropy/accuracy/rewind, backward tests, full cold
ABBA, external peak and all ten official checks remain mandatory.
